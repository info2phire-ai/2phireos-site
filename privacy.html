Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how 2phireOS ("2phireOS," "we," "our," or "us") handles information when the project is used to connect approved creator tools, automation services, and social media platforms.

1. Information we may process

Depending on the permissions granted by the user and the connected platform, 2phireOS may process account identifiers, profile information, authorization tokens, content selected for publishing, publishing status, basic analytics, and workflow metadata.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to provide requested creator workflow functions, including authenticating connected accounts, preparing or publishing user-selected content, organizing workflows, retrieving permitted account information, and troubleshooting integrations.

3. TikTok and third-party services

When a user connects TikTok or another third-party service, information is processed according to the permissions the user approves and the policies of that third party. 2phireOS does not claim ownership of a user's social account or content.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only when necessary to operate a requested integration, comply with law, protect security, or respond to a valid legal request.

5. Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the requested workflows, satisfy legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce agreements. Authorization may be revoked through the connected platform or integration provider.

6. Security

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect information. No online service or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. User choices and deletion requests

Users may disconnect their account through the relevant platform or integration settings. Requests concerning access, correction, or deletion may be submitted through the contact method below.

8. Children's privacy

2phireOS is not intended for children under the minimum age required by applicable law or the connected platform's terms.
